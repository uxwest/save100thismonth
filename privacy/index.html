Last updated October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information Save100ThisMonth (“we”, “the site”) collects when you visit save100thismonth.com, how it is used and the choices you have.

Where required by law, such as for visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK or certain US states, we will ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies, using a certified consent tool.

Your choices
