Best Prepaid Phone Plans: Cut the Bill Without Cutting Service

Your $70 postpaid line and a $30 prepaid line may ride the same tower — difference is priority at congestion plus hotspot and video footnotes. Most buy unlimited for under 10 GB use. Here is how to read real consumption, match prepaid structure, test free, and port safely.

1. Towers vs brands

Three physical networks; dozens of MVNO brands resell. Coverage = underlying network at your addresses. Postpaid gets priority in congestion; prepaid waits. Same bars most hours; slowdowns at stadiums, rush hour. If rarely on cellular at those moments, priority is unused premium.

Coverage is geography. Priority is timing.

2. Read real data use

iPhone: Settings, Cellular. Android: Network, Data usage. Average 3 months; separate Wi-Fi-heavy months. Most land 4–9 GB. Size for median; top-up outliers.

plan = median_GB x 1.25 buffer; unlimited only above ~25 GB sustained

5. Test coverage free

eSIM trials 7–30 days; carry a workweek; test elevator, garage, commute; speed-test 8am/noon/6pm. One home bar gap outweighs $20 savings — most find none.

6. Port safely

Unlocked + band-compatible (IMEI checker). Buy new, choose transfer number, give account + port PIN, wait activation before canceling old. Never cancel first.

Port first, cancel second.

7. Family math

Mix-and-match beats family discounts often: big bucket for heavy teen, small for parents. Start monthly; prepay after 3 happy months if refund sane.

8. When postpaid wins

Rural roaming fringes; reimbursed lines; hotspot-as-home-internet 50 GB+; flagship trade-in ladders climbed yearly. Else prepaid keeps towers, returns margin.
