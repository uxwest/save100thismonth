Best prepaid phone plans: the verdict for your data use

By Save100ThisMonth EditorialUpdated Oct 10, 202611 min read

Most people overpay for their phone plan because they buy far more data than they use. The right plan depends on one number: your real monthly consumption.

This guide gives the verdict by usage, then shows how to read the number and switch safely.

Read your real data use (five minutes)

iPhone: Settings > Cellular. Android: Settings > Network & internet > Data usage.

Average three full months and ignore any travel-dominated month. Most people land between 4 and 9 GB.

plan size = median monthly GB x 1.25 buffer example = 6 GB median -> about a 7.5 GB plan "unlimited" only makes sense above ~25 GB sustained

A "free" phone is paid for through higher monthly pricing and a lock-in. Buy an unlocked phone outright and pair it with cheap prepaid — it wins over three years.

Test coverage for free

Most carriers offer free eSIM trials of 7 to 30 days. Install one, carry it a full workweek and test home, the lift, the garage, the commute and weekend spots.

Speed-test at 8am, noon and 6pm. One bar of difference at home outweighs $20 a month.

Port your number safely

  1. Confirm your phone is unlocked and supports the new bands (IMEI checker).
  2. Buy the new plan and choose "transfer my number".
  3. Wait until the new line is active before cancelling anything.
Do not cancel first. Port first, cancel second. Cancelling releases the number.

FAQ

Is prepaid actually slower?

Off-peak, speeds are the same because it is the same network. The gap appears only during congestion.

Can I keep my number?

Yes. Port during activation. Do not cancel first.

How much data do I need?

Check 3 months of usage and size to the median with a buffer. Most people are fine under 10 GB.

Is a free phone worth it?

Only if you would keep that plan for the full term anyway. Run the three-year total against buying outright.
