Best Prepaid Phone Plans: Cut the Bill Without Cutting Service

Sub-keywords: cheap prepaid phone plans · MVNO vs carrier · unlimited data throttling · hotspot cap · eSIM trial · phone bill taxes

Quick answer: Most MVNOs ride Verizon/AT&T/T-Mobile towers at $25-45/mo instead of $70+. Check 3 months of real data use (most people <10GB), then compare deprioritization, hotspot allowance, video cap (often 480p) and taxes.
CheckWhy
Underlying networkCoverage = network, not brand
DeprioritizationPrepaid slows first at rush hour
HotspotOften capped or throttled
Multi-month discountReal price only if you prepay & stay

Method

  1. Settings → Cellular → read 3-month average.
  2. Size the plan to that number, not “unlimited”.
  3. eSIM free trial for coverage.
  4. Confirm phone unlocked before porting; never cancel old line first.
