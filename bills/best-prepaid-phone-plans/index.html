Best prepaid phone plans

By Save100ThisMonth EditorialUpdated Oct 10, 202611 min

Most people overpay for their phone plan because they buy far more data than they use. The right plan depends on one number: your real monthly usage.

Verdict by data use

Read your real data use

iPhone: Settings > Cellular. Android: Settings > Network & internet > Data usage. Average three full months; most people land between 4 and 9 GB.

plan = median GB x 1.25 buffer = 6 GB x 1.25 = ~7.5 GB plan "Unlimited" only makes sense above ~25 GB sustained.

Buy an unlocked phone outright and pair it with cheap prepaid — it wins over three years.

Test coverage free + port safely

Most carriers offer free eSIM trials. Carry one a workweek and test home, the garage, the commute and weekend spots.

  1. Confirm your phone is unlocked (IMEI checker).
  2. Buy the plan, choose "transfer my number".
  3. Wait until the new line is active before cancelling.
Do not cancel first. Port first, cancel second. Cancelling releases the number.

FAQ

Is prepaid slower?

Off-peak, speeds are the same. The gap appears only during congestion.

Can I keep my number?

Yes. Port during activation. Do not cancel first.

How much data do I need?

Check three months of usage and size to the median with a buffer.
