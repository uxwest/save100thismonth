Best Prepaid Phone Plans: Cut the Bill Without Cutting Service

Bills, Insurance & Telecom · Guide · For US readers · Plan prices and promos change constantly; verify on the carrier’s own site

Quick answer: Most major US carriers run prepaid or MVNO brands on the same towers as their expensive postpaid plans — often for $25–45/month instead of $70+. Switching a single line can save $300+ a year. The catch is deprioritization during congestion and locked-in quirks like hotspot limits, so match the plan to your actual data use.

The decision framework

  1. Check your last 3 months of actual data use (Settings → Cellular). Most people use under 10 GB.
  2. Pick a plan sized to that number, not to “unlimited” marketing.
  3. Test coverage with a free eSIM trial if the carrier offers one.
  4. Check your phone is unlocked and compatible before porting your number.
Honesty note: We don’t rank specific plans here because prepaid pricing changes monthly and coverage is intensely local. The framework is what stays true.

FAQ

Is prepaid service worse than postpaid?

Same towers, but prepaid traffic may be deprioritized in congestion. For most everyday use it’s indistinguishable.

Can I keep my number?

Yes — port it when you activate. Don’t cancel your old line first.
