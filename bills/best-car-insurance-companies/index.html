Best car insurance companies

By Save100ThisMonth EditorialUpdated Oct 10, 202612 min

There is no best insurer for everyone — rates move with your state, record and vehicle. But the right approach is clear: match the insurer type to your profile, then force identical coverage before comparing price.

Coverage, layer by layer

Liability pays other people's injuries and damage. A common frame is 100/300/100. State minimums are much lower; they keep you legal, not safe.

The three-quote method

  1. Freeze the package. Write down limits and deductibles. Do not change them while shopping.
  2. Collect 3-5 quotes — national, regional and an independent agent.
  3. Check quality via your state regulator or the NAIC, then financial strength.
  4. Ask for the renewal price before you commit.

Deductible math

break-even years = yearly saving / extra deductible = $180 / ($1,000 - $250) = 4.2 years You need ~4 claim-free years before the higher deductible pays off.

Only choose a deductible you could pay from savings tomorrow.

How to verify a quote

  1. Confirm it is licensed through your state department or the NAIC.
  2. Check the complaint record at the NAIC Consumer Information Source.
  3. Get the frozen coverage in writing and confirm the renewal price.

FAQ

Who has the cheapest car insurance?

It depends on your state, record and vehicle. Get three to five quotes with identical coverage; regional insurers are often cheapest for clean-record drivers.

Is the cheapest insurer worse at claims?

Not necessarily. Check complaint data and financial strength instead of assuming.

How many quotes should I get?

At least three with identical coverage, five for unusual profiles.
