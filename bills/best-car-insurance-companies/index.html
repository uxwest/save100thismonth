Best Car Insurance Companies: Why the Cheapest Quote Often Costs You Most

Sub-keywords: cheap car insurance quotes · full coverage vs liability · car insurance discounts · claims satisfaction · re-quote auto policy

Quick answer: Get 3-5 quotes with identical liability limits + deductible, then compare claims satisfaction (NAIC complaint index), financial strength, discounts you actually qualify for, and renewal price. State-minimum liability saves ~$15/mo and can ruin you after one bad crash.

The 3-quote method (apples to apples)

  1. Fix coverage first: e.g. 100/300/100 liability + $500 deductible + comp/collision if the car is worth it.
  2. Request that exact package from 3-5 insurers.
  3. Check each insurer at NAIC / your state DOI for complaints + strength.
  4. Ask explicitly for: bundling, safe-driver, low-mileage, telematics, paid-in-full, paperless — many are never auto-applied.

FAQ

Full coverage vs liability?

Liability pays others; comp/collision pays your car. Old paid-off car? Liability + emergency fund often wins.

How often to re-quote?

Every 12-24 months or after move / new car / new driver. Loyalty is frequently penalized.
