Emergency Fund Runway: how many months could you last?

Save100ThisMonth toolsUpdated October 2026Runs in your browser

Startups measure runway: how many months of cash before the money runs out. Your household has a runway too. Most people have simply never measured it.

Enter your savings and essential monthly costs. You will get your runway in months, a score, and the monthly amount needed to reach a safer target.

What counts as an essential cost?

Only what you truly could not skip for a few months. Be honest, not optimistic.

  • Rent or mortgage, utilities and basic phone service
  • Groceries, not restaurants
  • Insurance premiums, especially health and car
  • Minimum debt payments
  • Transport you need to look for work

Streaming, dining out and most subscriptions are not essential. Pausing them is part of the plan.

The formula

monthly burn = essential costs - income you would still have runway = accessible savings / monthly burn gap = (target months x monthly burn) - savings monthly plan = gap / months to reach target

Where should the money live?

Somewhere boring, liquid and insured. A high-yield savings account at an FDIC-insured bank is the usual answer. It pays interest and you can reach it within a few days.

Do not invest your runway. Markets can fall exactly when layoffs rise. Read our high-yield savings checklist before choosing an account.

Three ways to extend your runway fast

  1. Lower the burn. Every $100 cut from essentials adds runway without saving a cent.
  2. Automate the plan. Schedule the monthly amount the day after payday.
  3. Redirect windfalls. Tax refunds and bonuses can close the gap in one move.
Cutting costs extends your runway twice: you save more, and you burn less.

Frequently asked questions

Is 3 or 6 months better?

Three months suits stable jobs and dual-income households. Six or more suits single earners, freelancers and people in volatile industries.

Should credit cards count?

No. Available credit is debt waiting to happen. Count only cash you can actually access.

Is my data saved?

No. Everything stays in your browser.
