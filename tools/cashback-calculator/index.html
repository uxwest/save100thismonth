Cashback Comparison Calculator

Ads promise one card beats everything. Reality depends on how you actually spend. Enter your monthly mix and two rewards structures, and see which one genuinely pays more over a year — after any annual fee.

Press Compare to see the difference.

Formula: Card A = (groceries + dining and travel) at rate A, then everything else at a flat rate of 0 unless you set one. Card B = all spending at its flat rate. Annual fee is subtracted from each card's yearly earnings.

Limits. This ignores category caps, rotating categories, redemption minimums and promotional multipliers. Verify each card's current terms with the issuer, and never carry a balance to earn rewards — interest dwarfs any cashback.
