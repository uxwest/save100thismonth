Savings Interest Calculator

Tools · No data leaves your browser · Formula and limitations below

Enter your figures and calculate.

Formula: monthly rate r = APY converted to a monthly equivalent; balance grows as balance × (1 + r) each month. Interest = final balance − starting balance − deposits.

Assumptions & limitations: APY is variable and can change at any time; taxes on interest are not included; no additional deposits are modeled. Interest earnings are an estimate, not a guarantee.
