Subscription Savings Calculator

Annual plans look cheaper — until you cancel in month seven. This tool shows the yearly difference, the month at which annual billing breaks even, and an estimate of the cost over three years.

Press Calculate.

Formula: yearly saving = monthly × 12 − annual. Break-even month = annual ÷ monthly. Three-year estimate applies the expected rise to both plans equally.

Limits. Ignores refunds, mid-term increases beyond the estimate, and any extra fees for switching plans. Choose monthly when you are genuinely unsure whether you will keep the service.
