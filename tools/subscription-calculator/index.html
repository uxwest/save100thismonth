Subscription Savings Calculator

Tools · No data leaves your browser · Formula and limitations below

Enter both prices and calculate.

Formula: savings = (monthly × 12) − annual. The annual plan is cheaper only if you keep the subscription the full 12 months.

Limitations: ignores price increases mid-term, refunds and your chance of cancelling early. If you might cancel within the year, the monthly plan may be smarter even at a higher listed price. All figures are examples you provide — we don’t store or send anything.
