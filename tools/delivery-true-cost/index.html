Delivery true cost: what that order really cost you

Save100ThisMonth toolsUpdated October 10, 2026Runs in your browser

Delivery apps rarely show you one clean number. The menu price is often higher than in the restaurant, then a service fee, a delivery fee, tax and tip stack on top.

Plug in an order you placed recently. The calculator shows the true cost, how far it sits above the in-store price, and what that habit adds up to over a year.

Where the extra money goes

Each layer looks reasonable alone. Together they can push an order 50 to 80% above what you would pay at the counter.

Menu markup

Many restaurants raise app prices to cover the commission the platform charges them. Compare the app price to the restaurant menu and you will often see the gap.

Service and delivery fees

Usually a percentage plus a flat fee, and both can rise at busy times.

Tip

Tips matter to drivers and you should keep tipping fairly. Just note that a percentage tip is calculated on the already-marked-up subtotal.

The formula

app subtotal = menu price x (1 + markup) total = subtotal + service fee + delivery fee + tax + tip pickup total = menu price x (1 + tax) extra per year = (total - pickup total) x orders per month x 12

Smart ways to keep the convenience

  1. Order pickup in the same app. You skip delivery and service fees but keep the convenience of ordering ahead.
  2. Order directly from the restaurant. Many have their own ordering at menu prices.
  3. Batch orders. One larger order beats three small ones with three delivery fees.
  4. Check membership maths. A delivery pass only pays off above a certain order frequency.
Convenience is worth paying for. Just know the real price.

How we tested this tool

What we checked
  • Negative inputs are treated as zero, so a stray minus sign cannot create a fake saving.
  • Percentage fields are clamped: markup 0 to 100%, service fee 0 to 50%, tax 0 to 15%, tip 0 to 40%.
  • Orders per month is clamped to 0 to 60.
  • Hand-traced example: a $15 menu price with 20% markup, 12% service fee, $3.99 delivery, 7% tax and 18% tip totals about $26.12 against a pickup price of about $16.05.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the markup?

Compare the item price in the app with the restaurant website or in-store menu. The difference divided by the in-store price is the markup.

Should I tip less to save money?

No. Drivers rely on tips. Save by ordering less often or choosing pickup, not by cutting the tip.

Are these fees the same everywhere?

No. They vary by app, city, restaurant and time of day. Use numbers from your own receipt.
