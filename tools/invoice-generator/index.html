Free invoice generator

Runs in your browserNo signupNothing uploaded

Most “free” invoice tools want an account. Some want your client data. This one asks for nothing.

Fill the fields, build a preview, print to PDF. Refresh the page and every trace is gone, because nothing is ever sent or stored.

Privacy: this tool does not upload, store or transmit anything. It is a document template, not accounting or tax software. Check your own invoicing and tax requirements.

What makes a good invoice

  • A unique number. Sequential invoice numbers make bookkeeping and disputes far easier.
  • A date and a due date. “Net 30” means nothing unless the start date is on the document.
  • One clear description per line. Vague line items invite late payment.
  • Your payment details. Do not make the client hunt for how to pay you.
Getting paid fasterShorten the payment window for smaller jobs, send the invoice the same day you deliver, and follow up the day after it is due. Most late payment is forgetfulness, not refusal.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data saved anywhere?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser. Closing or refreshing the page erases everything.

How do I export the invoice?

Press Control or Command and P, then choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog.

Is this a substitute for accounting software?

No. It produces a document. It does not track payments, calculate tax or keep records. Use proper accounting software for that.

Can I add multiple line items?

This version handles one line. Use the description field for a combined item, or repeat the process per line for larger jobs.
