Best Free Website Builder for a Business: What “Free” Actually Costs

Subscriptions & Digital Services · Guide · For US readers · Free plans change often; verify each provider’s current plan page

Quick answer: “Free” builders exist, but for a real business they come with three catches: platform branding (ads or badges on your site), a subdomain instead of your own domain (yourbusiness.builder.com), and often payment-processing or feature limits. The genuinely free route that scales best for a simple business site is a static site on a free host (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages) plus a cheap domain — if you (or someone you know) can handle basic setup.

The three “free” routes

RouteWhat you getThe catchBest for
Free tier of a hosted builderDrag-and-drop editor, templates, hosting includedPlatform branding, subdomain, feature limitsTesting an idea before committing
Static site on a free hostYour own HTML or a site generator; custom domain allowed; fast and ad-freeYou handle setup and updates yourselfSimple brochure sites on a budget
Free CMS/social presenceBusiness profiles on Google, Facebook, marketplacesYou don’t own the platform or its rulesSupplementing, not replacing, a website

Hidden costs to add up

  • Domain: ~$10–20/year, unavoidable for credibility.
  • Payment fees: some free plans take a cut of e-commerce sales or block checkout entirely.
  • Removal of branding: often the exact feature locked behind the first paid tier.
  • Time: DIY setup is free in dollars, not in hours.
Honesty note: Free plan limits change constantly. Before choosing, read the provider’s current plan comparison page and ask specifically: “Can I connect my own domain, and is platform branding removed?”

Recommendation by situation

  • Just validating an idea: a free tier or even a one-page static site is perfect.
  • Local service business: a simple static one-pager + Google Business Profile beats an expensive builder plan.
  • Selling online from day one: budget for a paid e-commerce tier — free plans rarely process payments well.

FAQ

Is a subdomain bad for business?

It looks less professional and you don’t control it. For client-facing businesses, a custom domain (~$10–20/year) is the best small investment you can make.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Usually yes with hosted builders, but check export options — some lock your content format.
