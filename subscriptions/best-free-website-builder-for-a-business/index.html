Best free website builder for a business

By Save100ThisMonth EditorialUpdated Oct 10, 202610 min read

Free website builders genuinely exist, and for some businesses they are the right answer. But "free" always includes at least one of three catches: branding, a subdomain, or a limit where you need it most.

The three catches of free

What "free" actually includes
CatchesWhat it means
Platform brandingAds and badges on your site
Subdomainyourbusiness.builder.com, not yours
Payment limitsOften where the paid tier starts

Verdict by situation

When free works and when it does not
You…Best route
Validating an ideaA free tier or a one-page static site
Local service businessStatic one-pager + a real business listing
Selling online from day oneBudget for a paid e-commerce tier

Hidden costs to add up

  • Domain, around $10 to $20 a year
  • Removing platform branding (first paid-tier lock)
  • A 3% sales commission that becomes expensive the moment you sell
  • Your own time — free in dollars, expensive in hours
real annual cost = domain + renewal + payment fees + your hours note: a free plan with a 3% commission becomes costly the moment you sell anything

The single best small investment

Buy a custom domain. It is the cheapest possible upgrade in perceived legitimacy.

A business address ending in a builder's name reads as a hobby. Pointing a real domain at a free host removes the most visible signal that you are improvising.

A custom domain costs about the price of two coffees a year and changes how customers read everything else on the page.

The genuinely cheap route that scales

The static-host route is the only one that gives you a real domain, fast pages and no branding at no monthly cost. The trade is that you handle the setup, or pay someone once.

For a business that needs what you do, where you are, your hours and proof you are real, that trade is often excellent value.

FAQ

Is a subdomain bad for business?

It looks less professional and you do not control it. A custom domain is the best small investment.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Usually yes, but check export options — some lock your content format.

Do free plans include e-commerce?

Often partially, with a commission or without checkout. If selling is core, budget for paid.
