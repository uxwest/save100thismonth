Wix vs Squarespace for a portfolio

By Save100ThisMonth EditorialUpdated Oct 10, 202610 min read

This is less about features and more about temperament. One gives you a freeform canvas; the other gives you structure that is hard to make ugly.

Side by side

Price behaviourIntro discounts, full renewalFewer promos, predictable

One caution applies to both: your text and images export, but the design does not. Switching later means a rebuild.

The real cost over three years

Do not compare promotional prices. Take the renewal price, add a custom domain, and multiply by three.

3-year cost = (renewal x 3) + (domain x 3) + add-ons compare the RENEWAL, never the first-year discount

A plan that looks cheaper in year one often inverts by year three, because intro discounts expire. Run it through our subscription calculator.

Which suits which portfolio

Wix tends to win when

  • You want an unusual layout nothing in a template offers
  • You need multilingual pages
  • You enjoy tinkering and app-store extras

Squarespace tends to win when

  • Photography and design carry the brand
  • You want it to look considered without weekends of work
One asks what you want to build. The other asks whether you would rather it just looked right.

Before you commit

  1. Check current plan prices on the provider's own page.
  2. Confirm you can connect your own domain on your tier.
  3. Test the editor on a real page, not just a template preview.

FAQ

Can I switch platforms later?

Content exports; the design does not. Budget for a rebuild.

Is the free plan enough?

Free tiers add branding and a subdomain — fine for a draft, not clients.

Which is better for photographers?

Squarespace's gallery-first templates suit photography with less effort.
