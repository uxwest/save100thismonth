Wix vs Squarespace for a portfolio: which fits how you work?

By Save100ThisMonth EditorialUpdated October 10, 202610 min read

For a portfolio site, the platform decision is less about features than about temperament.

One gives you a freeform canvas that will do anything you can imagine, including look messy. The other gives you structured templates that are hard to make ugly and slightly harder to make unusual.

This guide compares them on the things that actually matter: design control, behaviour under your own habits and the real three-year cost once promotional pricing ends.

The real cost over three years

Do not compare promotional prices. Take each platform's renewal price for the plan you actually need, add a custom domain and multiply by three.

3-year cost = (renewal price x 3) + (domain x 3) + add-on fees compare the RENEWAL, never the first-year discount

A plan that looks a few dollars a month cheaper in year one frequently converges or inverts by year three, because introductory discounts expire while the renewal price does not.

Which one suits which portfolio

Wix tends to win when

  • You want an unusual layout nothing in a template library offers
  • You need multilingual pages, which Wix handles well
  • You enjoy tinkering and want to add functionality from an app store

Squarespace tends to win when

  • Photography and design work carry the brand, and typography matters most
  • You want the result to look considered without spending weekends on it
  • You would rather not fight the tool
One platform asks what you want to build. The other asks whether you would rather it just looked right.

Before you commit to either

  1. Check current plan prices on each provider's own pricing page. Both change regularly.
  2. Confirm you can connect your own domain on the tier you are considering. A portfolio on a subdomain looks like a draft.
  3. Test the editor on a real page during the trial, not just a template preview. That is where the daily friction lives.
  4. Note refund terms before paying annually, which is usually the only way to get the advertised rate.
  5. Decide how much design work you want to do yourself. This single question usually settles the choice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch platforms later?

Content exports from either. The design does not, so budget for a rebuild. This is the strongest argument for choosing carefully the first time.

Is the free plan enough for a portfolio?

Free tiers typically add platform branding and use a subdomain. Fine for a draft, not for showing clients.

Which is better for photographers?

Squarespace's gallery-first templates suit photography portfolios with less effort. Wix gives more control if you have a specific layout in mind and the patience to build it.

Do I need e-commerce?

Only if you sell prints or services directly. Squarespace's built-in commerce is cleaner for simple catalogues; Wix's marketplace is broader if you need integrations.

How much should I budget annually?

Price the plan you need at renewal, not the discounted first year, and add a custom domain.
