Wix vs Squarespace for a Portfolio: Which One Fits How You Work?

Subscriptions & Digital Services · Comparison · For US readers · Pricing changes; verify on each company’s pricing page before subscribing

Quick answer: Choose Wix if you want maximum layout freedom and don’t mind a busier editor. Choose Squarespace if you want polished, gallery-first templates that look professional with less fiddling. Both charge similar annual prices (typically $150–300/year at verified checkout — check current plans); the difference is design freedom vs. design discipline. For most portfolios, Squarespace wins on looks, Wix wins on control.

The real cost over 3 years

Don’t compare promo prices. Take each platform’s renewal price for the plan you actually need, add a custom domain (~$10–20/year), and multiply by 3. A plan that looks $3/month cheaper can cost the same or more by year two. Run the numbers in our subscription calculator.

Honesty note: Pricing and features change frequently. Verify current plan prices and included features on each company’s own pricing page — lists like this one go stale quickly.

When each one wins

  • Wix wins: unusual layouts, multilingual sites, app-store add-ons, very specific visual ideas.
  • Squarespace wins: photography and design portfolios where typography and image presentation carry the brand, and you want it to look great with minimal tweaking.

FAQ

Can I switch platforms later?

Content (text, images) exports fine; your design does not. Budget for a rebuild if you switch.

Is the free plan enough for a portfolio?

Free plans put platform branding and ads on your site with a subdomain — fine for a draft, not for showing clients.
