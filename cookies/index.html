Cookie policy

Cookies are small files a site stores in your browser. Here is what we use, and what we do not.

What we use today

Cookie categories
CategoryUsed now?Purpose
Strictly necessaryMinimalThe site is static and works without cookies. No login, no shopping cart.

Our calculators

All of our tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us, and the tools set no cookies of their own.

When advertising is enabled

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Where required by law, we will ask for consent before setting non-essential cookies, using a certified consent tool.

How to control cookies

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking some cookies may affect advertising relevance, and blocking all of them will not break this site's articles or tools.
