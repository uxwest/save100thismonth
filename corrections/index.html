Corrections

When we get something wrong, we say so, fix it and log it here.

How we handle corrections

  • Material errors are corrected in the article itself, with a note describing what changed.
  • Substantive changes are logged below, newest first.
  • Readers can report errors through our contact page. We aim to review within a few business days.

Correction log

No corrections logged yetThis log is maintained from launch. Every entry will state the page, the date and what changed.

Why we publish this page

A corrections log is one of the clearest signals that a publication takes accuracy seriously. It also means we cannot quietly rewrite history. If a figure was wrong, the record shows it.
