Fact-Checking Policy

  • Prices, rates, fees and terms are checked against the provider's official site at the time of writing.
  • Time-sensitive figures carry a note telling readers to verify before deciding; we include a last-checked date where the data is central to the article.
  • Claims we cannot verify are either removed or explicitly flagged as unverified — never presented as confirmed.
  • Independent testing labs and regulators (e.g., FDIC, NAIC/state insurance departments, state licensing boards) are cited where relevant.
  • Illustrative examples are labeled as examples and use clearly stated assumptions.

Found an error? Contact us — see also our Corrections Policy.
