Fact-checking policy

What we verify, and where

Claim type and source
Claim typePrimary source we check
Deposit insurance limitsFDIC published rules and BankFind
Savings and CD ratesThe bank's own rate and fee disclosures
Insurance rules and complaintsState departments of insurance and the NAIC
Trading rules and taxesSEC investor bulletins and IRS publications
Inflation figuresBureau of Labor Statistics CPI releases
Software, cards and subscriptionsThe provider's own pricing, terms and help pages

Our rules

  • Any number that changes frequently is either verified at publication or clearly marked as illustrative.
  • Claims we cannot verify are removed or explicitly flagged. They are never presented as confirmed.
  • Examples use stated assumptions and are labelled as examples.
  • Where a topic carries risk of loss or harm, the limitation is stated in the article, not buried.

Found an error?

Tell us at corrections@save100thismonth.com with the page URL and a source. We aim to review within a few business days and log material changes on the corrections page.
