Best day trading platform, and an honest risk warning

By Save100ThisMonth EditorialUpdated Oct 10, 202611 min read
Read this first. Trading is risk-taking, not saving. Treat any trading capital as money you can afford to lose entirely. To grow savings reliably, start with our savings guide instead.

Most finance sites will not write this: most active traders lose money after costs. If you still want to proceed, at least choose the platform with clear eyes.

The Pattern Day Trader rule, first

A US margin account that makes 4+ day trades in 5 business days must keep at least $25,000 in equity. Below that, the broker restricts further day trades.

This is regulation, not a policy you can negotiate. A small account cannot legally day trade frequently. Any platform suggesting otherwise is selling a limit you will hit by your fourth trade.

Zero commission is not free

A "zero commission" broker still earns from your activity, and you still pay costs that never appear on a commission line.

all_in_cost = commissions + options fees + regulatory fees + (data subscription x months) + (average spread x shares traded) spreads widen exactly when you are most tempted to trade
  • Regulatory fees on sales
  • Per-contract fees on options
  • Monthly data subscriptions you forget to count
  • The bid-ask spread, the largest hidden cost

Execution quality: the factor nobody compares

Two brokers can show the same price and give different fills. Order routing, payment for order flow and fast-market handling all affect what you pay. On a volatile open, execution is the difference between a strategy that works and one that bleeds.

Taxes surprise most people

Short-term gains are taxed as ordinary income, not the lower long-term rate. Wash-sale rules can disallow losses if you rebuy within 30 days. And high trade counts make reporting painful.

A saner default

For money you want to grow over years, a diversified low-cost portfolio plus an insured emergency fund covers most people well. Our online banks guide handles the savings side, and the runway calculator shows your cushion.

If you trade anyway. Paper trade 30 days first. Set a hard monthly loss limit and stop when you hit it.
