Best online banks

By Save100ThisMonth EditorialUpdated Oct 10, 202611 min

There is no single best online bank, but there is a right type for your banking style. Here is the verdict, then how to verify FDIC and fees.

Fees that eat your interest

On a $2,000 balance, a 4.25% rate earns about $85 a year. A $5 monthly fee takes $60 of it.

net = (balance x new rate) - (balance x old rate) - yearly fees = $85 - $0.20 - $60 = roughly $24.80

Subtract fees from interest. That is the only comparison that matters.

FDIC, decoded

FDIC insurance covers deposits to $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category.

  1. Confirm it is an insured bank on FDIC BankFind.
  2. Your account is held there, not just marketed by an app.
Ceiling alert. Above $250,000 at one bank, the extra is uninsured.

The 30-minute switching method

  1. Shortlist five: two online banks, one big bank's arm, one credit union, one fintech app.
  2. Copy the facts from each bank's own page: APY, fees, transfer rules.
  3. Score them, fees and insurance double-weighted.
  4. Act. Open the winner, test a small transfer, automate a deposit.

FAQ

Are online banks safe?

Yes, if FDIC-insured. Verify on BankFind.

Do I need both online and traditional?

Many keep both: online for savings, a branch or credit union for cash.

Is a fintech app a real bank?

Often not. Safe only if the app names the insured bank behind it.
