Best High-Yield Savings Accounts: How to Compare Before You Move Your Cash

Quick answer: Pick the FDIC-insured account with a competitive sustained APY, $0 monthly fees, and access that fits you. Never pick on headline rate alone — promos expire and fees erase interest.
On this page: criteria · 30-min method · mistakes · FAQ

30-minute method

  1. Shortlist 4–6 FDIC-insured banks (verify at fdic.gov).
  2. Check each bank's own site for APY + fee schedule.
  3. Check rate history for promo vs standard.
  4. Read outgoing-transfer fees.
Honesty note: No frozen ranking — APYs change weekly. This framework stays valid. Try the savings calculator with a rate you verify.

FAQ

Is a high APY guaranteed?

No — variable rates move with the market.

Are online banks safe?

If FDIC-insured, up to $250,000 per depositor/category/bank.
