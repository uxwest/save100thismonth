Editorial Policy

Our approach

  • Frameworks over rankings. Where prices and rates change constantly, we publish comparison criteria and point you to primary sources, instead of frozen top-10 lists.
  • Primary sources first. Rates, fees and terms are verified on the provider's own website whenever possible.
  • Honest labeling. We distinguish facts, estimates, examples and opinions. Illustrative numbers are marked as such.
  • No invented anything. No fake authors, testimonials, statistics or results. Author bylines appear only for real, named contributors.
  • No guarantees. We never promise savings, returns, earnings, traffic or approvals.
  • Timeliness flags. Guides that depend on changing data carry a verify-before-deciding note and, where applicable, a last-checked date.
  • Affiliate transparency. Commercial links, when present, are disclosed per our Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure and never influence our criteria.

Updates

Guides are reviewed and updated when we detect material changes. Corrections are logged on the Corrections page.
