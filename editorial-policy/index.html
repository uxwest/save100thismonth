Editorial policy

How this site is researched, written and maintained, and where advertising stops and editorial begins.

Our standards

  1. Frameworks over rankings. Where prices and rates change constantly, we publish comparison criteria and point to primary sources instead of frozen top-10 lists.
  2. Primary sources first. Figures come from the provider's own disclosures or from regulators and public bodies such as the FDIC, the NAIC, the SEC, the IRS and the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
  3. Honest labelling. Facts, estimates, examples and opinions are labelled as such. Illustrative numbers are never presented as current prices.
  4. No invented content. No fabricated authors, testimonials, statistics or results. Bylines appear only for real contributors.
  5. No guarantees. We never promise savings, returns, earnings, approvals or outcomes.
  6. Time sensitivity. Guides that depend on changing data carry a note telling readers to verify before deciding.
  7. Advertising separation. Commercial relationships never influence comparison criteria, and sponsored content is clearly labelled.

Use of AI in our process

We may use software tools for drafting, structure and checks. Every published page is reviewed by a human editor, figures are verified against primary sources and anything that cannot be verified is removed or flagged.

Financial topics

Money and insurance content is written as general information for US readers. It is not financial, insurance, legal, tax or medical advice, and it does not consider your personal circumstances. For decisions with real consequences, speak to a licensed professional in your state.

Updates and corrections

We review guides when we detect material changes and correct errors in the article, with substantive corrections logged on the corrections page. To report an error, see contact.
