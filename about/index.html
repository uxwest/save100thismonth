About Save100ThisMonth

Independent guides and free tools for households in the United States that want to spend smarter without the hype.

What we publish

Long-form comparisons and educational guides on savings, banking, insurance, phone plans, travel cards, subscriptions and software. Every guide shows how to compare, explains the arithmetic and links to primary sources.

We also publish free calculators that run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored.

What we do not do

  • We do not publish invented prices, statistics or rankings.
  • We do not name a single “best” product when rates change weekly and a stale list misleads.
  • We do not promise savings, returns or any financial outcome.
  • We are not financial, insurance, legal or medical advisors. Our content is general information.

How we work

  1. Frameworks, not frozen lists. Where data changes constantly, we teach the comparison method and point you to primary sources.
  2. Verification first. Figures come from the provider's own disclosures or regulators such as the FDIC, the NAIC or the IRS.
  3. Honest labelling. Illustrative examples are marked as illustrative. Nothing is presented as a quotation of current pricing unless it is one.
  4. Corrections in public. Material errors are corrected in the article and logged on the corrections page.
  5. Transparency about money. Advertising may fund this site. It never influences comparison criteria.

The name

“Save 100 this month” is an aspiration, not a claim. Some people save more by switching a phone plan. Others do not save anything, because the right answer for their situation is to spend more on something that matters. We are not going to pretend otherwise.
