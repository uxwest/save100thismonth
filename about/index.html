About Us

Save100ThisMonth is an independent editorial site about smart spending for US readers. Our mission: help you get more value from your money — better purchases, better accounts, fewer wasted subscriptions — without austerity or clickbait.

What we are

  • A comparison and education publication, funded in part by advertising and, when applicable, clearly disclosed affiliate links.
  • Editors of guides that explain how to compare, with criteria and sources, rather than publishing rankings we can't verify.

What we are not

  • We are not financial, insurance, legal or medical advisors. Our content is general information.
  • We never promise savings, returns, traffic or results. $100 this month is an aspirational goal, not a guarantee.

Our standards

See our Editorial Policy, Fact-Checking Policy, and Corrections Policy. If you spot an error, tell us — we fix things publicly.

Status

This site is in launch phase. Author pages and bylines will be added as the editorial team is confirmed — we do not publish invented author credentials.
