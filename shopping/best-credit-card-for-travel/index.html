Best Credit Card for Travel: Pick by Your Trips, Not the Points

Points feel like wealth until redemption: blackouts, portal-only credits, devaluations. Travelers who profit share one habit — fee-versus-value math on their own trips. This guide walks it line by line: the three card families, realistic point values, FX fees, lounge audits, fine print, and downgrade triggers.

1. The three families

Transferable points: flexible currency for planners who compare award charts. Co-brands: one airline, perks (bag, boarding, companion). No-fee travel: no FX fee + flat rewards, zero upkeep. Most belong in family one or three; family two pays only for monthly loyalists.

3. Realistic point values

Cash 1c; portal 1–1.5c; smart transfers 1.2–2c+ premium cabins early. Under 1.5c average = cash-back card wins. Points devalue; earn to spend, not collect.

Points are currency the issuer can devalue overnight.

4. FX fees non-negotiable

3% on $5,000 abroad = $150 lost. Travel card must charge 0%. Always pay in local currency; check cash-like exclusions.

5. Lounge audit

Two trips = ~2 visits x $30 = $60 vs $395 fee. Under ~8 visits/yr lounges rarely justify premium alone. Count last year first.

7. Cash back crossover

Flat 2% no-fee no-FX is the rational default. Points win only with high spend + planned transfers on flown airlines. Simplicity compounds.

8. Application sequence

Space applications; keep oldest open; downgrade not cancel; never carry balance for rewards — one month at 20% APR erases a year of points.

FAQ

Points or cash back?

Above ~1.5c planned transfers: points. Else cash back.

Airline or flexible?

Loyal + bags: co-brand. Variety: flexible.

Acceptable FX fee?

Zero for travel cards.

Start premium as beginner?

No — start no-fee no-FX; upgrade when math proves.
