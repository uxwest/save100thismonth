Best Credit Card for Travel: Pick by Your Trips, Not the Points

Sub-keywords: travel rewards card · transferable points · airline co-brand · no foreign transaction fee · lounge access value · annual fee math

Quick answer: Flexible traveler → transferable-points card. One-airline loyalist → co-brand (free bag + boarding). 1-2 trips/yr → no-fee, no-FX-fee card. Value = credits you'd use anyway + lounge visits + points at YOUR redemption + FX savings − annual fee.

Before you apply (issuer's own page)

  1. Annual fee + welcome offer + minimum spend you can meet responsibly.
  2. Foreign transaction fee (must be $0 if you travel abroad).
  3. Transfer partners you actually fly.
  4. Credit fine print: portal-only? hotel-specific? expiring?
  5. Never carry a balance for points — interest dwarfs rewards.
