Best Credit Card for Travel: Pick by Your Trips, Not the Points

Quick answer: Loyal to one airline? Co-brand may win. Flexible? Transferable-points card wins. Fly twice a year? No-fee card usually wins.

The math

Real value = credits you'd use anyway + lounge value + points at your redemption + FX savings. Compare against annual fee. Never carry a balance for points — interest dwarfs rewards.

Hook: A $395 card is free if you use the credits. Otherwise it's just $395.
