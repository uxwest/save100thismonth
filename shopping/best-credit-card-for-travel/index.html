Best travel credit card

By Save100ThisMonth EditorialUpdated Oct 10, 202611 min

There is no single best travel card, but there is one correct answer for you, and it depends on how you travel. Here it is, then the maths to avoid overpaying.

The fee-versus-value equation

Add only the value you would actually use, then subtract the annual fee.

net = usable credits + lounge value + (points x your rate) + foreign fee savings - annual fee If it's negative two years running, downgrade.

Use two of the three — credits, lounge, status — and it pays. Use none and you paid for a metal ornament.

Foreign fees: non-negotiable

A 3% foreign fee on $5,000 of overseas spend costs $150 a year. Any card you carry abroad must charge 0%.

  • Always pay in the local currency
  • Check ATM policies abroad
  • Confirm "no foreign fee" covers cash-like use too

How to verify the offer

  1. Identify your family from the table.
  2. Open the issuer's page for the current fee and bonus.
  3. Confirm three deal-breakers: 0% FX, credits you will use, a bonus from normal spending.

FAQ

Are points better than cash back?

Only above about 1.5 cents per point with planned transfers. Below that, cash back wins.

Airline card or flexible card?

One airline with regular bags favors a co-brand. Booking by price favors flexible or no-fee.

What foreign fee is acceptable?

Zero, for any card you use abroad.
