Hidden fees and markups

  • App menu prices often 20 to 30% above the restaurant
  • Service fee + delivery fee on top
  • Tip calculated on the inflated subtotal
The quiet trap. A delivery subscription can make ordering feel free and increase how often you order.

The hybrid that actually sticks

  1. Batch cook on a normal week. Default mode.
  2. Keep one kit for known chaos.
  3. Allow delivery once a month, deliberately. A treat, not a fallback.

FAQ

Are meal kits cheaper than groceries?

Rarely per serving. Their value is time saved and waste avoided.

How do I cancel a meal kit?

Find the weekly cutoff in your account and pause before it. That cutoff is the most important detail.

Is delivery-app food more expensive?

Usually yes. Compare app and menu prices, then add fees and tip. Picking up costs much less.
