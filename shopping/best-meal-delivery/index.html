Best meal delivery: the verdict for how you actually cook

By Save100ThisMonth EditorialUpdated October 10, 202610 min read

There is no best meal delivery for everyone — it depends entirely on how much time and energy you have on a given week. This guide gives the verdict by lifestyle, the real cost per serving, and the hidden fees that decide the total.

The fastest answer, right now
  • You have time to cook: groceries + batch cooking — by far the cheapest per serving.
  • You hate planning and waste, but will cook: a meal kit — costs more than groceries, buys you the plan.
  • You have no time to cook at all: prepared meals — most expensive after delivery apps, saves all prep.
  • You order out as a treat, not a habit: keep delivery apps to once a month — they are the most expensive per meal once fees and tips are counted.

Cutting delivery from four nights a week to once a month is one of the largest single levers in a normal food budget.

Hidden fees and markups

  • Menu markup. App prices are often 20 to 30% above the restaurant menu.
  • Service fee. Usually a percentage.
  • Delivery fee. Varies by distance and demand.
  • Small-order fee. Below a threshold the app chooses.
  • Tip calculated on the inflated subtotal.
  • Delivery subscriptions that only pay off above a certain frequency — and can raise it.

Meal kits

Kits solve deciding what to cook and shopping for it. If those are why you do not cook, the per-serving premium buys something real. Before committing, find the skip-week deadline, read what happens if you miss it and confirm the post-promotion price.

Prepared meals

Prepared meals also remove the cooking, so they cost more per serving. They make sense when time is genuinely scarce or diet requirements would otherwise mean ordering out. Watch for portions, subscription minimums and the "fresh vs frozen" price gap.

The hybrid that actually sticks

  1. Batch cook on a normal week. Default mode.
  2. Keep one kit for knew-it-was-coming chaos.
  3. Allow delivery once a month, deliberately. A treat, not a fallback.

This keeps the savings without demanding the discipline that makes strict plans collapse.

Frequently asked questions

Are meal kits cheaper than groceries?

Rarely per serving. Their value is time saved and waste avoided. It depends on how you behave, not the sticker price.

How do I cancel a meal kit?

Find the weekly cutoff in your account and pause or cancel before it. That cutoff is the most important detail for any subscriber.

Is delivery-app food more expensive?

Usually yes. Compare app and menu prices, then add service fee, delivery fee and tip. Picking up often costs much less.

Is a delivery subscription worth it?

Only if order frequency justifies the fee, and only if it does not make you order more often.

What is the biggest saving here?

Reducing how often you order delivery. It is the most expensive per meal once all fees are counted.
