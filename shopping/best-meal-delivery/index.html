Best Meal Delivery: Kits vs. Prepared vs. Restaurant Apps

Smart Shopping · Comparison · For US readers · Prices vary by market and week; always check the service’s own checkout for current costs

Quick answer: Meal kits usually cost more per serving than cooking from scratch but less than restaurant apps — and they cut food waste. Prepared meals cost more per serving than kits but save all prep time. Restaurant delivery is almost always the most expensive per meal once fees and tips are added. The “best” depends on how much your time is worth and how many people you feed.

Worked example (illustrative)

4 dinners/week, 2 people: batch cooking might run roughly $4–6 per serving in ingredients; kits often $9–13; prepared meals $10–15; restaurant delivery $18–28 after fees and tip. Multiply the difference by 16 dinners/month and you can see where a $100+/month gap appears — verify current pricing in your area before making decisions.

Tips that actually reduce the cost

  • Rotate: use kits for busy weeks, batch-cook for normal weeks, and drop restaurant delivery to once a month.
  • Check cancellation policy and the post-promo price before any “first box” deal.
  • Compare the app’s menu price against the restaurant’s own price — you’re often paying a silent markup.

FAQ

Are meal kits cheaper than groceries?

Rarely per serving. Their value is time saved and waste reduced — decide if that trade is worth it for you.

How do I stop a meal kit subscription?

Pause or cancel before the weekly cutoff date shown in your account; that date is the single most important detail to find.
