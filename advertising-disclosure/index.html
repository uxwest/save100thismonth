Advertising and affiliate disclosure

Running this site costs money. Here is how it is paid for, in plain terms.

How we may earn

Funding sources
SourceWhat it means
Display advertisingThird-party ads shown alongside content. Advertisers do not choose or approve our editorial.
Affiliate linksIf we link to a provider and you sign up, we may receive a commission. It costs you nothing extra and does not change our criteria.
SponsorshipsPaid content, always labelled as sponsored at the top of the page.

What never changes because of money

  • Comparison criteria. Fees and insurance are weighted the same whether or not a provider pays.
  • The decision to recommend against something. We publish limitations even when a partner dislikes it.
  • Corrections. Errors are fixed regardless of who is involved.

Ads and cookies

We may display advertising served by Google and its partners. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings, and you can read more in our privacy policy.

Current status

No advertising network is active on this site yet. Ad slots are reserved in the layout but contain no ad code until approval. No affiliate links are currently in use.
